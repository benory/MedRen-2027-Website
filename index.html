The Medieval and Renaissance Music Conference
comes to KU Leuven in the summer of 2027.

Venue

The conference will take place on the premises of KU Leuven’s Faculty of Arts.

Blijde Inkomststraat 21
3000 Leuven, Belgium

Dates and schedule

The conference will have a leisurely start on Monday, 5 July 2027, allowing delegates to travel to Leuven that day and avoid potential issues with Rock Werchter (1–4 July 2027).
