MedRen Leuven 2027

The Medieval and Renaissance Music Conference comes to KU Leuven, Belgium, from 5–9 July 2027.

MedRen Leuven 2027 is organised by David J. Burn, Antonio Chemotti, and Bart Demuyt, in collaboration with the Department of Musicology at KU Leuven, the Alamire Foundation, and the Royal Library of Belgium (KBR).

As in previous years, MedRen welcomes proposals on a wide range of topics relating to medieval and Renaissance music. For the 2027 edition, we would also like to particularly encourage submissions engaging with a number of themes connected to the research interests and activities of the organising partners. These themes include:

  • Music for the Dead — music, death, mourning, and commemoration
  • Chansons and Chansonniers — repertories, sources, transmission, performance, and reception
  • Musical Life in the Low Countries in a Global Context — musical practices, communities, networks, and the circulation of musicians, repertories, and ideas
  • Technological Approaches to Music and Music Sources — digitisation, databases, digital editions, virtual acoustics, computational methods, and other technological approaches to the study, documentation, preservation, and interpretation of music and musical sources
  • Music Sources at the Royal Library of Belgium (KBR) — manuscripts, prints, provenance studies, and new approaches to the study of musical sources
  • Museums, Collections, and the Curatorship of Musical Heritage — the collecting, preservation, display, and mediation of musical objects and heritage, as well as curatorial practices and new approaches to presenting music and its histories to diverse audiences

These themes are intended as points of departure rather than exclusive categories, and proposals on other topics relating to medieval and Renaissance music are equally welcome.
